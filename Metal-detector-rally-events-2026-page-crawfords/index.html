🧠 Thinking About Your Detector?

Rallies are the best place to see machines working in real UK soil. Many detectorists decide on their next upgrade after a rally conversation, not a spec sheet.

This is where brands fade into the background and detecting takes centre stage. You'll see all makes working side-by-side, and that's exactly how it should be.
